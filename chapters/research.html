The constraint

Freecash, Almedia’s rewards app, serves 60 million users, and Amplitude holds the story of how they move. But a funnel only opens up if you know every event name and how the taxonomy is built.

For a designer that meant waiting for an analyst, or guessing.

So I connected Claude to Amplitude, and the taxonomy stopped being the gate.

From answers to layouts

For My Offers, Voicepanel answers and Customer Support notes went to Claude for analysis.

With AI agents, we brainstormed new layouts against the problems it found.

Five versions reached Figma, real enough to put in front of users.

  • Confirmed by community

    V3 as the base direction

    Both Reddit and Discord independently landed on V3. Build from here.

  • Confirmed by community

    Progress bars + %, but applied selectively

    Most praised addition across both channels.

  • Confirmed by community

    Ability to hide / dismiss offers

    Users are stuck with irrelevant or completed offers blocking the list.

  • Strong signal

    Surface the next locked bonus reward

    Currently completely invisible: users are flying blind.

  • Strong signal

    Play Rewards before Burning Rewards

    Play rewards are the most frequent activity; they should lead.
